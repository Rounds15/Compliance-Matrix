Syracuse Compliance Matrix

One record of the University's regulatory obligations, who owns them, when they are due, and the risk they carry.

Office of Compliance and Enterprise Risk Management

The Compliance Matrix captures the University's regulatory obligations and connects each one to the law behind it, the people accountable, the deadlines that drive it, and the risk it carries. It replaces scattered spreadsheets with one current, searchable system.

[[metric:owners]]

Named Owners

Accountability assigned at the executive, unit, and compliance level for each obligation.

Compliance Calendar

Deadlines tracked on a centralized calendar with automated reminders at 90, 30, and 0 days. Owners are notified weekly once a deadline is overdue. Completions are recorded and recurring deadlines roll forward automatically.

[[metric:deadlines]]

Deadlines tracked

[[metric:statutes]]

Governing statutes mapped

Compliance Assessments

A rolling review of controls on a one to five year cycle. Owners receive a questionnaire, responses are scored, and gaps are identified and tracked to closure. Results are reported to leadership.

[[metric:executives]]

Executive owners

Accountability Structure

Each obligation names its owners at the executive, unit, and compliance level, with General Counsel and support roles where applicable. Ownership is tracked and verified so accountability stays current.

Report a Concern

Any member of the University community can flag a record for the Office of Compliance to review. Flags enter a review queue and the office responds within five business days.
